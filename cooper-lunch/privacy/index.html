Privacy Policy

Cooper Lunch Alerts · Last updated November 18, 2026

This policy explains what Cooper Lunch Alerts, operated by Anthony Compofelice (Around The Woodlands, The Woodlands, Texas), collects when you sign up for text messages and how it is used.

What we collect

  • Your mobile phone number.
  • The date and time you agreed to the consent statement on the sign-up form, and which version of that statement you saw.
  • Your subscription status (pending, active or stopped) and the dates it changed.

We do not collect your name, email, address, your child's information, location, or payment details. The sign-up page sets no cookies and uses no analytics or advertising trackers.

How we use it

Only to send Cooper Lunch Alerts messages, to confirm your sign-up, to honor STOP and HELP requests, and to keep a record of your consent and opt out.

Sharing

No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Text messaging originator opt-in data and consent will not be shared with any third parties. Your number is processed by Twilio, the messaging provider that delivers the texts, solely to deliver them. We may disclose information only if required by law.

Storage and security

Records are stored in the operator's Twilio account, which is protected by account credentials and accessible only to the operator.

Retention and deletion

Active subscribers' records are kept while you are subscribed. After you reply STOP, a record that your number opted out is kept so it is not texted again. Reply DELETE at any time to erase your number and sign-up record immediately; you will receive one final confirmation text and no further messages.

Children

The program is intended for parents and guardians. It is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their information.

Changes

Updates to this policy will be posted on this page with a new date.

Contact

Reply HELP to any Cooper Lunch Alerts message, or text +1 (833) 756-2455.
