Cooper Lunch Alerts

A free text each school night with the next day's lunch menu, so you can decide on packing lunch the evening before.

Cooper lunch Wed, Nov 11 - Turkey Pot Pie - Fresh Baked Rolls - Side Salad Veg: Buffalo Cauliflower Salad Bowl

How it works

What you get
One text each school night (Sunday to Thursday) around 6:30 PM Central with the next day's lunch and vegetarian option.
How often
Up to 5 messages per week. No texts on weekends or school breaks, apart from one heads up before a closure.
Confirming
After you sign up you get one text asking you to reply YES. Menus start only after you confirm.
Stopping
Reply STOP to any message to stop all texts. Reply DELETE to erase your number. Reply HELP for help.
Cost
Free. Message and data rates from your carrier may apply.

About

Cooper Lunch Alerts is run by Anthony Compofelice, a parent in The Woodlands, as part of Around The Woodlands. Menus come from the school's published monthly menu and can change without notice.
