SMS Terms and Conditions

Cooper Lunch Alerts · Last updated November 18, 2026

1. The program

Cooper Lunch Alerts ("the program") is a free, noncommercial text message service operated by Anthony Compofelice, a sole proprietor doing business as Around The Woodlands, The Woodlands, Texas. Subscribers receive one text message each school night (Sunday through Thursday) from +1 (833) 756-2455 with the next day's lunch menu from The John Cooper School. The program is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or operated by The John Cooper School.

2. Signing up is optional

You join only by entering your own mobile number on the sign-up form, checking the consent box, and then replying YES to the confirmation text, or by texting YES or START to +1 (833) 756-2455. Signing up is entirely optional. It is not required to buy anything, to use any other Around The Woodlands app or website, or to receive any service from the school. Consent to receive texts is not a condition of any purchase.

3. Message frequency

Up to 5 messages per week, plus one confirmation text when you sign up and one reply when you opt out or ask for help. No messages are sent on weekends, holidays or school breaks, except one notice before a closure.

4. Cost

The program is free. Message and data rates may apply according to your mobile carrier plan.

5. How to stop

Reply STOP to any message at any time to cancel. STOPALL, UNSUBSCRIBE, CANCEL, END and QUIT also work. You will receive one final message confirming you have been unsubscribed and will receive no further messages. To rejoin, reply START or YES, or sign up again on the form. Reply DELETE to erase your number and sign-up record.

6. Help

Reply HELP to any message for program information.

7. Delivery

Carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages. Menu content comes from the school's published menu and may change without notice; always confirm with the school if a meal matters for allergies or dietary needs.

8. Your number

Sign up only a mobile number that you own or are authorized to use. If you change or give up your number, reply STOP from it first.

9. Privacy

See the Privacy Policy. No mobile information is shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes.

10. Changes

The program may be changed or ended at any time. Material changes to these terms will be posted on this page and announced by text.
